Assessment FAQ

What if my child is very shy?
Our therapists are trained to build rapport at the child's pace. We use play-based warming-up periods to ensure your child feels safe and comfortable before we begin any formal tasks.
Can you review my child's current school report?
Absolutely. School reports are a critical part of understanding how your child functions in an academic and social environment. We incorporate these insights into our final clinical summary.
How should I explain the visit to my child?
We recommend telling them they are going to "meet a new friend to play some games and show them all the cool things they can do." Avoid words like "test" or "doctor" if your child has medical anxiety.
What happens if my child has a meltdown during the session?
Meltdowns are a form of communication. Our therapists are equipped to manage these moments with empathy and can often gain valuable clinical insight into your child's sensory processing and regulation from how they respond to stress.
How much does an assessment cost?
Assessment fees depend on the type and depth of evaluation your child needs. For the current fee and exactly what's included, please call or WhatsApp us — we'll give you a clear, no-obligation answer before you book anything.
How long does the assessment take, and when will we see progress?
The initial assessment usually takes 60–90 minutes, and you'll receive a detailed written report within about 10 working days. Therapy timelines vary from child to child — many families notice early changes within the first few months, and we review goals regularly so progress stays measurable.
Do you provide school consultation and support?
Yes. We can review school reports, share practical classroom strategies with teachers, and provide written recommendations to support your child's learning and inclusion at school.
