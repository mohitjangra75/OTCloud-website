Dr. Dheeraj founded OTCloud with a clear purpose: to help families understand their child's needs before making decisions about therapy. That principle still shapes how every assessment, plan and review is carried out here.

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

Creating Inclusive Schools Where Every Child Thrives

To become one of India's most trusted Child Development Partners, supporting schools in creating inclusive environments where every child is understood, every educator is empowered, and every family is actively engaged in the child's developmental journey.

Our Mission

Transforming Understanding into Meaningful Action

We collaborate with schools through teacher development, parent empowerment, early identification, multidisciplinary expertise, and evidence-based child development practices to build stronger educational communities and create measurable progress for every child.
