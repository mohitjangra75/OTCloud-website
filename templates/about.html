The Visionary Behind OT Cloud

Dr. Dheeraj Suthar

A Master of Occupational Therapy (Neurosciences) with over a decade of clinical experience in pediatric rehabilitation.

Dr. Dheeraj founded OT Cloud on one firm conviction — that children make the greatest progress when specialists work as one coordinated team, and when parents are treated as true partners in the journey. Frustrated by fragmented, one-therapist-at-a-time care, he built an integrated model where occupational therapy, speech, physiotherapy, special education and psychology come together under a single roof.

Today he leads a multidisciplinary team that has supported hundreds of families across Gurugram — pairing evidence-based practice with genuine warmth, and the belief that every child deserves to be understood before they are ever expected to change.

"Every child deserves the opportunity to be understood before they are expected to change. Our team's job is to decode the 'why' behind the 'what'."

Frequently Asked Questions

How are therapists assigned to my child?
Following the initial assessment, our clinical lead assigns therapists based on your child's specific profile and the therapist's area of special interest and expertise.
Will I always see the same therapist?
Consistency is key for therapeutic rapport. We aim for full consistency, though assistants may occasionally support sessions under senior supervision for intensive programs.
How does the team share information about my child?
We use a centralized secure digital system and hold monthly multidisciplinary clinical rounds where every child's progress is discussed across departments.
How long will my child need therapy?
It varies with each child's needs and goals. Many families see meaningful change within a few months, and we review progress regularly so therapy continues only as long as it's genuinely helping.
How many sessions a week are recommended?
Most children benefit from 1–3 sessions per week. The exact number is recommended after the initial assessment, based on your child's profile and goals.
