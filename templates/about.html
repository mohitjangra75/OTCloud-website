Frequently Asked Questions

How are therapists assigned to my child?
Following the initial assessment, our clinical lead assigns therapists based on your child's specific profile and the therapist's area of special interest and expertise.
Will I always see the same therapist?
Consistency is key for therapeutic rapport. We aim for full consistency, though assistants may occasionally support sessions under senior supervision for intensive programs.
How does the team share information about my child?
We use a centralized secure digital system and hold weekly multidisciplinary clinical rounds where every child's progress is discussed across departments.
