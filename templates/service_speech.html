What Is Speech & Language Therapy?

Speech and language therapy helps children develop the skills needed to communicate and interact with others.

Communication is more than speaking. It can include understanding what others say, using words and sentences, answering questions, expressing needs, having conversations, using gestures and participating socially.

A speech-language therapist looks at the different parts of communication and identifies the skills that can be supported through individualized therapy.
