Common Questions

Everything you need to know about starting your journey with OT Cloud.

Do I need a doctor's referral?
No referral needed. You can contact us directly to schedule an intake assessment for your child.
How long does the initial assessment take?
It takes 45 to 60 minutes, including child observation and a brief discussion with parents.
Can both parents attend the session?
Yes, absolutely. We encourage both parents to join for a better understanding of routines.
What age groups do you cater to?
We support children from early infancy up to 14 years of age.
What types of therapy do you provide?
We offer Occupational, Speech, Behavioral, and Sensory Integration therapies.
How soon will we see progress in our child?
Most parents notice positive developmental changes within 6 to 8 weeks of regular sessions.
What happens after the initial assessment?
We share a detailed evaluation report and a personalized therapy roadmap for your child.
Can we get home strategies or activities to practice?
Yes, we provide custom home activity plans to support progress between sessions.
