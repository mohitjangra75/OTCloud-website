What is Special Education?

A specialized pedagogical approach designed to meet the unique learning needs of children who require additional support. At OT Cloud, it isn't just about 'finishing homework' — it's about building the cognitive architecture for lifelong learning through evidence-based, multisensory strategies.

Learning Is More Than Academics

Attention

Focus, sustain engagement, and filter distractions during complex tasks.

Understanding

Processing and making sense of abstract concepts and instructions.

Memory

Working memory and recall strategies to retain and use information.

Problem Solving

Cognitive flexibility to find solutions when faced with obstacles.

Communication

Expressing ideas clearly and understanding social-academic exchange.

Organisation

Managing materials, time, and sequential tasks efficiently.

Independence

The confidence and skill to complete tasks without constant prompting.

The Assessment Process

1

Initial Consult

Reviewing clinical history, teacher reports, and parental concerns.

2

Direct Testing

Standardised and informal assessments of literacy, numeracy, and cognition.

3

Observation

Assessing focus, frustration tolerance, and learning style in real time.

4

Analysis

Synthesizing data to identify specific neuro-educational gaps.

5

The Report

A comprehensive roadmap including goals and school recommendations.

6

Feedback

Detailed discussion with parents to align on the intervention path.

Our Teaching Approach

Play-Based Learning

Using intrinsic motivation to embed academic concepts in enjoyable, low-pressure activities.

Visual Supports

Leveraging diagrams, schedules, and icons to reduce cognitive load and enhance understanding.

Multisensory Methods

Engaging touch, sound, and movement to reinforce neural pathways for literacy and math.

Scaffolded Instruction

Breaking tasks into micro-steps, gradually fading support as independence increases.

School Readiness Programme

Designed for children aged 4–6, this programme ensures the transition to primary school is seamless and confident.
