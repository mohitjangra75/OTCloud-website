Tailored Therapy Programmes

Foundation

Weekly support for consistent skill building.

Best for mild or single-area delays (e.g. early speech or fine-motor), and maintaining gains after intensive therapy.

  • 1 Session per week
  • Basic Home Programme
  • Monthly Reviews
Enquire Now
Recommended

Growth

Balanced support for steady developmental progress.

Best for active sensory-integration needs and children working across two or more areas (e.g. speech + attention).

  • 2–3 Sessions per week
  • Detailed Intervention Plan
  • Fortnightly Feedback
  • School Support Guidance
Enquire Now

Intensive

High-frequency therapy for accelerated results.

Best for significant or multiple developmental challenges (e.g. ASD, global delay) needing rapid, closely-coordinated progress.

  • 4+ Sessions per week
  • Holistic Case Management
  • Intensive Home Support

Message from the Founder

Dr. Dheeraj Suthar

Founder-Director and Senior Occupational Therapist
Master of Occupational Therapy — Neurosciences
10+ Years of Clinical Experience

I founded OTCloud with a clear purpose: to help families understand their child's needs before making decisions about therapy.

Parents deserve more than a list of concerns or a standard treatment plan. They deserve a careful assessment, honest guidance, coordinated professional support and a clear understanding of their child's progress.

At OTCloud, our responsibility is not only to provide therapy. It is to help every child develop meaningful skills for everyday life while giving families the clarity and confidence to move forward.

"Every child possesses unique potential. Our role is to understand their needs and help that potential emerge."
