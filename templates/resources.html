Myth vs Fact

We're here to debunk common misconceptions and replace anxiety with evidence-based facts.

Submit a Question
Myth:

"Boys always talk later than girls, it's just their nature."

Fact:

Developmental timelines are similar across genders. Waiting to see if they 'grow out of it' can miss critical early-intervention windows.

Myth:

"Therapy is only necessary after a formal diagnosis."

Fact:

Support can begin the moment a challenge is identified, often helping children overcome hurdles before they become barriers.

Common Questions

How do I know if my child needs an assessment?
If you've noticed consistent difficulty with skills your child's peers are mastering, or your parental intuition is signaling a concern, an assessment provides a professional baseline.
Is Occupational Therapy just for physical issues?
Not at all. OT also addresses sensory processing, emotional regulation, executive functioning, and social-emotional skills.
How long does an assessment process take?
Typically, an initial assessment lasts 60–90 minutes, with a comprehensive report provided within 10 business days.

Understanding Before Intervention

While the resources in our Learning Centre are designed to inform and empower, please remember that online information does not replace individual clinical assessment. Every child is unique, and professional observation is the only way to create a tailored therapy plan that works.
