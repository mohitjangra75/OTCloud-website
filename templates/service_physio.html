What Is Child Physiotherapy?

Child physiotherapy focuses on helping children develop the physical skills needed to move, play, participate and become more independent in everyday activities.

A pediatric physiotherapist looks at how a child moves and identifies areas where support may improve strength, balance, coordination, posture, mobility and functional participation.
